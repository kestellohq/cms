# Code of conduct

Kestello is built by people with different backgrounds, experience and first languages. We want taking part to be a good experience for every one of them.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, in issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and anywhere else the project lives.
- Assume good intent, and ask before assuming.
- Give feedback about the work, never the person. Explain the reason behind a request for changes.
- Accept that a decision may go against you, and that maintainers have the final say on what goes into Kestello.
- Help newcomers find their way.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, slurs, or personal or political attacks.
- Unwelcome sexual attention or content.
- Discrimination of any kind.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or encouraging any of the above.

## Reporting

If something here happens to you or you see it happen, tell the maintainers privately: use the contact address on the [kestellohq organization profile](https://github.com/kestellohq), or message a maintainer directly. Reports are kept confidential. A maintainer who is part of the report takes no part in handling it.

## What maintainers may do

Depending on what happened: talk with the person, ask them to change or remove what they wrote, edit or remove it themselves, issue a warning, or suspend or ban them from the project's spaces for a time or for good.

## Scope

This applies in every project space (the repositories, discussions, chat and events) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

This code of conduct draws on the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
